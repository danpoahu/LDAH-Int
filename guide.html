Tier 1 — Quick Start

What Is LDAH-Int

LDAH-Int is the internal staff dashboard for Leadership in Disabilities & Achievement of Hawai'i (LDAH). It is the central place where authorized staff members manage day-to-day operations, including:

  • Tracking interactions and case work with contacts
  • Reviewing and responding to website form submissions (provider requests, pledges, event requests, contact messages)
  • Managing events, programs, and signup registrations
  • Editing website content through a visual CMS (Content Management System)
  • Coordinating volunteers and their applications
  • Maintaining community resource listings, FAQs, team/board member profiles, and photo galleries
  • Viewing analytics, generating reports, and communicating with other staff via built-in chat

The dashboard is a web application accessed through your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Firebase for authentication, data storage, and file uploads.

Logging In

When you first open LDAH-Int, you will see a login screen.

  1. Enter the email address associated with your staff account.
  2. Enter your password. If this is your first time, your administrator will have sent you an invite with instructions to set up your password.
  3. Click Sign In.
Forgot your password? Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen. A reset email will be sent to your address. If you still cannot log in, contact your administrator.

After signing in, you will land on the main Dashboard view.

Navigation & Sidebar

The left sidebar is your primary navigation. It is organized into three groups:

GroupSections
Dashboard Main Dashboard (KPIs, alerts, recent activity), Interactions, Contacts
Admin CMS Dashboard, Team & Board, Gallery, Resources, FAQ, Events & Programs, Volunteers, Website Data, User Roles, List Management, Audit Log, Reports, Analytics, Resource Activity, Change Requests, Chat
Website (CMS) Page Editor (WYSIWYG) for each website page

At the bottom of the sidebar you will find a tips area that displays rotating helpful hints about using the dashboard. These tips are managed by administrators through the List Management section.

Sidebar visibility: The sections you see depend on your user role. For example, only SuperAdmin and Admin users can access User Roles and the Audit Log.

Key Areas at a Glance

  • Dashboard — Your home base. KPI cards give you a snapshot of totals across the system. Click any card to jump directly to that section. You will also see recent interactions, upcoming events, and the notification bell for new submissions.
  • Interactions — The core case-tracking tool. Create and manage records of every interaction LDAH has with contacts, including notes, status tracking, and export.
  • CMS (Page Editor) — A visual, what-you-see-is-what-you-get editor for updating content on the public LDAH website. Click any element to edit it, then publish when ready.
Tier 2 — Daily Use

Checking the Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after signing in. It provides a high-level overview of all activity across the system.

  • KPI Cards — Each card shows a count (for example, the number of active pledges, volunteer applications, or events). Click any card to jump directly to the corresponding section. This is the fastest way to navigate.
  • Recent Interactions — A summary of the most recently created or updated interactions.
  • Upcoming Events — Events approaching in the near future.
  • Weather Alerts — Dismissible banners based on alert severity. You can configure your weather preferences in your profile settings.
  • Partner Island Local Times — A scrolling ticker showing the current local time for LDAH's partner islands across the Pacific.

Notification Bell

The bell icon in the top navigation area shows a badge count when there are new, unreviewed items from the public website. Clicking the bell opens a dropdown listing:

  • New provider requests
  • New anti-bullying pledges
  • New event requests
  • New contact messages
  • New event signup registrations

Click any individual alert to navigate directly to that item in the relevant section. Once you view or act on the item, the alert clears from the bell.

Presence Strip

At the top of the page, a presence strip displays small avatars or initials for each staff member who is currently online. This lets you see at a glance who is active in the system right now. Presence updates automatically; users go to "away" after 10 minutes of inactivity and "offline" when they close the dashboard or sign out.

Managing Interactions

Interactions are the backbone of LDAH case tracking. Here is the typical daily workflow:

  1. Go to Interactions in the sidebar.
  2. Click New Interaction to create a record. Select the contact, the communication channel (phone, email, in-person, etc.), the interaction type, and add notes describing what happened.
  3. Set the initial status — typically Open for a new case, or Draft if you want to finish it later. Draft interactions appear with a counter badge on the navigation item so you do not forget them.
  4. As the case progresses, update the status to Needs Review or Closed.
  5. To add follow-up notes to an existing interaction, open it and use the Add Note field. Each note is appended with your username and a timestamp. Notes have a 500-character limit per entry.
  6. Use the filter controls at the top to narrow the list by status, date range, contact, or interaction type.
  7. Use the Export CSV button to download interaction data for reporting.

Checking Website Submissions

Public visitors submit forms on the LDAH website. Those submissions flow into the Website Data section of the dashboard.

  1. Navigate to Website Data in the sidebar.
  2. Use the tabs at the top to switch between: Provider Requests, Anti-Bullying Pledges, Event Requests, and Contact Messages.
  3. Each submission is shown as a card. Click to expand and see full details.
  4. Use the status workflow buttons on each card to move the item through its lifecycle (for example, New → Reviewed → Contacted for provider requests).
  5. Use the Admin Notes field to record internal notes about the submission. Admin notes are for staff only and are not visible to the public.
  6. Use the Export CSV button on any tab to download that category of submissions.

Viewing Event Signups

  1. Navigate to Events & Programs in the sidebar.
  2. Find the event or program you want to check.
  3. Click View Signups on the event card.
  4. For one-time events, you will see a list of all registrants with their details and any answers to custom questions.
  5. For recurring programs, signups are session-based. You will see a 30-day session view. Each session has individual cancel checkboxes so you can mark cancellations per session without affecting other dates.
  6. Use Export CSV to download signup data. Use Archive Signups to move past signups out of the active view.

Quick CMS Updates

To make a quick edit to the public LDAH website:

  1. Go to the Page Editor section in the sidebar (under Website / CMS).
  2. Click the tab for the page you want to edit (Home, Contact, Volunteer, Events, Who We Are, Resources, etc.).
  3. The editor shows a visual preview of the page. Click on any text or image element to select it for editing.
  4. For text, a rich text toolbar appears with options for bold, italic, underline, lists, links, colors, and sizes.
  5. Changes auto-save as you work. You will see a green "Saved" indicator confirming your edits are stored.
  6. When you are satisfied, click the "Publish to Website" button in the publish bar that appears at the top. Your changes will go live on the public site immediately.
Drafts vs. Published: Auto-saved changes are drafts until you click Publish. Other staff will see a publish bar indicating there are unpublished changes. Only click Publish when the content is ready for the public.
Tier 3 — Detailed Reference

The main Dashboard is organized into several panels:

At the top of the Dashboard, a row of KPI (Key Performance Indicator) cards displays live counts for major data categories:

CardWhat It Shows

Each card is clickable — clicking it takes you directly to the corresponding management section.

Recent Interactions Panel

Below the KPIs, a panel shows the most recently created or updated interactions with a summary of the contact name, date, channel, and current status.

Upcoming Events

A panel listing events that are approaching, sorted by start date. Helps staff prepare for upcoming activities.

Weather Alerts

When active weather alerts affect Hawai'i, they appear as dismissible banners on the Dashboard. Alerts are color-coded by severity. You can configure which severity levels you want to see in your profile settings.

Partner Island Local Times

A scrolling ticker at the top of the sidebar displays the current local time for each of LDAH's partner islands across the Pacific (American Samoa, CNMI, FSM, Guam, Marshall Islands, Palau). Hover over the ticker to pause it.

Contacts

The Contacts section stores information about every individual and organization LDAH works with.

Viewing Contacts

Contacts are displayed as a searchable list. Use the search bar to filter by name, contact type, or organization.

Adding or Editing a Contact

Only users with Admin or SuperAdmin roles can add or edit contacts.

  1. Click Add Contact (or click the edit button on an existing contact card).
  2. Fill in the available fields:
    • Name — First and last name
    • Email — Primary email address
    • Phone — Phone number
    • Organization — The organization the contact is affiliated with
    • Location — Geographic location (island or address)
    • Grant / Program — Associated grant or program, if applicable
  3. Click Save.

Contact History

Each contact card shows a history of all interactions associated with that contact, giving staff a complete picture of the relationship over time.

Interactions

Interactions are the primary record-keeping tool for LDAH staff. Each interaction documents a communication or case activity with a contact.

Creating a New Interaction

  1. Navigate to Interactions in the sidebar.
  2. Click New Interaction.
  3. Select the contact from the dropdown (or search by name).
  4. Choose the channel — how the interaction occurred (Phone, Email, In-Person, Video Call, etc.). Channels are managed through List Management.
  5. Choose the interaction type — what category the interaction falls under (Inquiry, Referral, Follow-Up, etc.). Types are also managed through List Management.
  6. Enter notes describing the interaction.
  7. Set the status:
    • Open — Active, needs follow-up
    • Closed — Resolved, no further action needed
    • Needs Review — Flagged for supervisor or team review
    • Draft — Incomplete, saved for later
  8. Click Save.

Draft Counter

When you have draft interactions, a badge counter appears on the Interactions navigation item in the sidebar. This serves as a reminder that you have unfinished work to complete.

Adding Notes to an Existing Interaction

Open any interaction and scroll to the notes section. Type your follow-up note in the Add Note field and click the add button. Each note is automatically appended with your username and a timestamp. Individual notes have a maximum length of 500 characters.

Filtering and Search

Use the Advanced Filters panel at the top of the Interactions list to narrow results by:

  • Status (Open, Closed, Needs Review, Draft)
  • Date range
  • Contact name
  • Channel
  • Interaction type

Exporting

Click Export CSV to download the current filtered view of interactions as a comma-separated values file. This is useful for generating reports or sharing data with external systems.

CMS Dashboard

The CMS (Content Management System) Dashboard gives administrators a bird's-eye view of all website content managed through the dashboard.

KPI Overview

Similar to the main Dashboard, the CMS Dashboard shows KPI cards specifically for website content: team member count, board member count, resource count, FAQ count, event count, and more.

Quick Action Buttons

Four quick-action buttons let you jump straight into common content tasks:

  • Add Team Member — Opens the Team & Board section with the add form ready
  • Create Event — Opens the Events & Programs section with the add form ready
  • Add Resource — Opens the Community Resources section with the add form ready
  • Add FAQ — Opens the FAQ section with the add form ready

Team & Board Management

This section manages the staff and board member profiles displayed on the public Who We Are page of the LDAH website.

Two Tabs

Switch between Team Members and Board Members using the tabs at the top of the section.

Adding a Member

  1. Click Add Team Member or Add Board Member.
  2. Fill in the fields:
    • Name — Full name as it should appear on the website
    • Title — Job title or board position
    • Phone — Contact phone (team members only)
    • Email — Contact email (team members only)
    • Photo — Upload a profile photo. Maximum file size is 5 MB. Square images are recommended for best appearance.
    • Bio — A brief biography, 2–3 sentences recommended
  3. Click Save.

Reordering

Each member card has a drag handle in the top-right corner. Click and drag to reorder members. The order you set here is the order they appear on the public website.

Archiving and Restoring

To remove a member from the public website without permanently deleting their record, click Archive. Archived members can be restored at any time by toggling the archive view and clicking Restore.

Gallery Management

Two photo galleries are managed through this section:

  • Gallery 1 — Photos displayed on the Who We Are page
  • Gallery 2 — Photos displayed on the Volunteer page

Adding Photos

  1. Select the gallery tab (Gallery 1 or Gallery 2).
  2. Click Add Photo and select an image file. Maximum file size is 5 MB.
  3. The photo will appear in the gallery grid.

Reordering

Drag photos to reorder them. The order here matches the display order on the public website.

Archiving

Click the archive button on any photo to remove it from the public gallery. Archived photos can be restored later.

Community Resources

The Community Resources section manages the directory of organizations and services listed on the LDAH website's Resources page.

Adding a Resource

  1. Click Add Resource.
  2. Fill in the fields:
    • Name — Organization or resource name
    • Type — Category of resource
    • Services — Description of services provided
    • City — City where the resource is located
    • Island — Select from: Oahu, Maui, Kauai, Hawaii Island, Molokai, Lanai, or Statewide
    • Website URL — Link to the organization's website
    • Logo — Upload a logo image for display
  3. Click Save.

Searching

Use the search bar to filter resources by name, type, city, or services.

Exporting

Click Export CSV to download the full resource directory as a spreadsheet file.

FAQ Management

FAQs on the LDAH website are organized by category. This section lets you manage both the categories and the individual questions within them.

Categories

FAQs are grouped into collapsible categories. To add a new category, click Add Category and enter the category name. Categories can be reordered by dragging.

Adding a FAQ

  1. Expand the category where the FAQ belongs.
  2. Click Add FAQ within that category.
  3. Fill in the fields:
    • Category — Pre-selected based on where you clicked, but can be changed
    • Question — The question as it will appear on the website
    • Answer — Rich text editor for the answer. You can use formatting (bold, italic, links, lists).
    • Action Steps (optional) — A numbered list of steps the reader can take. These are displayed separately from the main answer.
  4. Click Save.

Reordering

Within each category, drag FAQs to reorder them. The display order on the website matches what you set here.

Archiving and Restoring

Archive individual FAQs to remove them from the public site. Archived FAQs can be restored at any time.

Exporting

Click Export CSV to download all FAQs. If a FAQ has action steps, each action step appears on a separate row in the export file, grouped under its parent question.

Events & Programs

LDAH manages two types of activities:

  • One-Time Events — Activities with a single, specific date (workshops, conferences, fundraisers)
  • Ongoing Programs — Recurring activities with a weekly schedule pattern (support groups, regular classes)

Creating an Event or Program

  1. Click Create Event or Create Program.
  2. Fill in the fields:
    • Title — Name of the event or program
    • Description — Detailed description for the public listing
    • Image or PDF Flyer — Upload a promotional image or PDF flyer. Maximum file size is 10 MB.
    • Start Showing Date — The date when this event should begin appearing on the public website
    • Move to Past Date — The date when this event should automatically move to the "past events" section
    • Remove Date — The date when this event should be removed from public view entirely
  3. Configure signup options:
    • Date/Time Choices — Define available time slots for registration
    • Custom Questions — Add questions that registrants must answer (text fields, dropdowns, etc.)
    • Individual or Group Toggle — Whether registrants sign up individually or can register a group
  4. Click Save.

Programs: Weekly Schedule and Sessions

For ongoing programs, you define a weekly schedule pattern (for example, "Every Tuesday and Thursday, 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM"). The system then generates a 30-day session view showing each upcoming session date.

Staff can mark individual sessions as cancelled using cancel checkboxes per session. Cancelled sessions are shown with a visual indicator, and registrants for that specific session are notified.

Viewing Signups

Click View Signups on any event card to see all registrations. For programs, signups are organized by individual session date.

Admin Notes on Signups

Each signup record has an Admin Notes field for internal comments. These notes are visible only to staff and are not shared with registrants.

Exporting and Archiving

  • Export CSV — Download signup data for any event or program
  • Archive Signups — Move past or completed signups to an archive. Archived signups can be viewed separately.

Volunteer Management

The Volunteer section has two tabs: Opportunities and Applications.

Opportunities Tab

Manage the volunteer opportunities listed on the public Volunteer page.

  1. Click Create Opportunity.
  2. Fill in the fields:
    • Title — Name of the volunteer position or opportunity
    • Description — What the role involves
    • Image — Optional photo for the listing
    • Requirements — Any prerequisites or qualifications
    • Dates — When the opportunity is available
    • Show Always toggle — When enabled, the opportunity appears on the website regardless of its dates. Useful for ongoing or year-round positions.
  3. Click Save.

Admin Notes on Applications

Each application has an Admin Notes field for recording internal observations, interview feedback, or follow-up tasks. These notes are not visible to applicants.

Exporting

Click Export CSV to download applications. The export is grouped by opportunity, making it easy to see all applicants for each position.

Page Editor (WYSIWYG)

The Page Editor is a visual, what-you-see-is-what-you-get content editor that lets staff update the public LDAH website without writing code.

Available Pages

The following pages can be edited through the Page Editor:

  • Home
  • Contact
  • Volunteer
  • Events
  • Who We Are
  • Resources
  • Readiness
  • Special Ed
  • Military
  • Pacific — includes 6 island sub-pages:
    • American Samoa
    • CNMI (Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands)
    • FSM (Federated States of Micronesia)
    • Guam
    • Marshall Islands
    • Palau
  • Community

Editing Content

  1. Select a page tab at the top of the editor.
  2. The editor loads a visual preview of the page. Click any editable element (text block or image) to select it.
  3. For text elements, a rich text toolbar appears above the selected area with the following tools:
    • Bold, Italic, Underline — Standard text formatting
    • Lists — Bulleted and numbered lists
    • Links — Insert or edit hyperlinks
    • Colors — Change text color
    • Sizes — Adjust font size
  4. For image elements, clicking opens options to upload a replacement image.
  5. Changes auto-save continuously. A green "Saved" indicator confirms your edits are stored as a draft.

Publishing

When there are unpublished changes, a publish bar appears at the top of the editor. Click "Publish to Website" to push all saved changes to the live public website. Until you publish, changes remain as drafts visible only within the dashboard.

Important: Publishing is immediate. Once you click "Publish to Website," the changes are live and visible to the public. Always review your edits carefully before publishing.

Partner Role Access

Users with the Partner role have restricted access to the Page Editor. They can only view and edit their assigned island sub-page (for example, a Guam partner can only edit the Guam page under Pacific). They cannot access other pages.

Change Requests

Certain fields in the system are protected and cannot be edited directly by all staff. Instead, staff members submit a change request.

How It Works

  1. When you attempt to edit a protected field, a change request form appears instead of an inline editor.
  2. Submit your proposed change with an explanation of why it is needed.
  3. An Admin or SuperAdmin reviews the request.
  4. The reviewer can approve (the change is applied automatically) or reject (optionally including a note explaining why).
  5. All approved changes are recorded in the Audit Log.

Managing Users

  1. Navigate to User Roles in the sidebar.
  2. To add a new user, click Add User. Enter their name, email, assigned role, and location.
  3. To change an existing user's role, click the edit button on their card and select a new role.
  4. To remove access, click Archive. The user will no longer be able to sign in. Archived users can be restored if they return.

Status Indicators

Each user in the list shows a status indicator:

  • Online — Currently active in the dashboard
  • Away — Signed in but inactive for more than 10 minutes
  • Offline — Not currently signed in

To add an item to a list, select the list, type the new value, and click Add. To remove or reorder items, use the controls on each list entry.

Sidebar Tips

The rotating tips that appear at the bottom of the sidebar are also managed here. You can:

  • Add new tips
  • Remove outdated tips
  • Reorder tips to control the rotation sequence

Tips are shown to all users and rotate automatically in the sidebar footer area.

Audit Log

The Audit Log is accessible to SuperAdmin and Admin users. It provides a complete, chronological record of every administrative change made in the system.

What Is Recorded

  • Who made the change (username)
  • When the change was made (date and time)
  • What was changed, including:
    • The specific field that was modified
    • The before value (what it was)
    • The after value (what it was changed to)

The audit log is read-only and cannot be edited or deleted. It serves as the system's official record for accountability and compliance purposes.

Reports

The Reports section provides tools for generating and downloading various reports from the system.

Available Reports

  • Interaction Reports — Summaries of interaction activity by date range, contact, status, or type
  • Custom Exports — Select specific fields and filters to build a tailored report

Reports can be exported as CSV files for use in spreadsheets or other tools.

Profile & Settings

Access your profile by clicking your name or avatar in the top-right corner of the dashboard.

Editable Fields

  • Display Name — How your name appears to other staff in the dashboard
  • Email — Your login email address
  • Location — Your island or office location
  • Profile Photo — Upload a photo (maximum 2 MB). This appears in the presence strip and chat.
  • Emergency Contact Info — Name and phone number of your emergency contact
  • Weather Alert Preferences — Choose which severity levels of weather alerts to display on your dashboard

Resource Activity

The Resource Activity section is a file management area for documents shared within the organization.

Supported File Types

You can upload and manage the following file types:

  • PDF documents
  • Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls)
  • Word documents (.docx, .doc)
  • CSV files
  • PowerPoint presentations (.pptx, .ppt)

Uploading Files

Click Upload or drag and drop files directly into the upload area. Maximum file size is 25 MB per file.

Viewing and Managing

  • Click any file to view or download it
  • Use filters to narrow the file list by type, date, or uploader
  • Use Export to download a summary of all uploaded resources

Chat

The built-in Chat feature allows real-time messaging between staff members.

Using Chat

  • Open Chat from the sidebar navigation
  • Select a staff member to start or continue a conversation
  • Type your message and press Enter or click Send

Presence Indicators

Each staff member's chat entry shows their current status:

  • Online — Currently active
  • Away — Signed in but inactive
  • Offline — Not currently in the system

Users are automatically set to away after 10 minutes of inactivity and to offline when they close the dashboard or sign out.

Chat Logs and Administration

  • Admin users can view chat logs for accountability purposes
  • Purge old chats — Messages older than 13 months can be purged. Before purging, the system generates a CSV backup of all messages being removed so that records are preserved.
