LDAH Internal Dashboard — User Guide

A friendly walkthrough of the staff dashboard. No technical background needed.

How this guide is organized. Tier 1 (Quick Start) gets you logged in and oriented in five minutes. Tier 2 (Daily Use) covers the things you will likely do every day. Tier 3 (Full Reference) goes section-by-section through every feature, so you can look up anything when you need it.
Tier 1 — Quick Start

What Is LDAH-Int

LDAH-Int is the internal dashboard the LDAH staff use to run day-to-day work. Open it in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, or Edge all work) and sign in with your LDAH email. From one place you can:

  • See who has signed up for events, classes, and Connect-Gen sessions
  • Track every conversation and contact (Interactions)
  • Reply to website form submissions
  • Update what families see on the public LDAH website
  • Send announcement and reminder emails
  • Chat with other staff in real time
It just runs in your browser. Nothing to install, no app to download. Bookmark the page so you can find it again. It works on a desktop, an iPad, or a phone (some screens are easier on a larger device).

Logging In

  1. Open the dashboard in your browser.
  2. Type your LDAH email and your password, then click Sign In.
  3. If it's your first time, you'll see a quick orientation. Otherwise you'll land on the Dashboard.

Forgot your password?

Click Forgot Password on the sign-in screen. Type your email and you'll get a reset link. Open the link, choose a new password, and you're back in.

Trouble signing in? The most common cause is a typo in the email. Double-check the spelling. If you still cannot get in, ask La'akea or another superAdmin to confirm your account is active.

Navigation & Sidebar

Everything is reachable from the left sidebar. The sidebar groups items into three tabs at the top:

  • Main — Dashboard, Contacts, Interactions, Reports, Resource Activity
  • Admin — User Roles, Audit Log, Change Requests, List Management, Recipient Lists
  • CMS — Everything that updates the public website (events, FAQs, team, gallery, resources, page editor)

Click a tab to swap which group of items shows. The dashboard area on the right always stays in place; only the sidebar list changes.

What you'll see depends on your role

Some sections only appear for certain roles. For example, only superAdmins see User Roles. If a coworker says "go to X" and you don't see it, that's usually a role thing — not a bug.

Getting Help (AI Helper)

Look in the bottom-left corner for a small chat bubble and a question-mark icon. The question-mark opens the AI Helper — a built-in chatbot that knows the dashboard and can answer "how do I…" questions in plain English.

Examples of things to ask:

  • "How do I cancel one date for a Learning Lab?"
  • "Where do I find who signed up for last week's session?"
  • "How do I add a new community resource?"

The little blue "i" circles you'll see throughout the dashboard are field-level help — click any one for a short explanation of just that field or button.

Tier 2 — Daily Use

Checking the Dashboard

Your Dashboard is the home screen. It is designed so a quick scan tells you what needs your attention today. From top to bottom:

  • Weather widget — top-right, shows the current temperature for your assigned island. Click it to open a multi-island weather panel.
  • Notification bell — turns red when something new came in.
  • Presence strip — small avatars showing which other staff are online right now.
  • KPI tiles — quick counts of pledges, volunteer apps, signups, etc. Click any tile to jump straight to that list.
  • Today's Session Sheet — if any classes or events are happening today, you'll see them here with confirmed/pending counts.
  • Recent Interactions — last 10 contacts staff have reached out to.
  • Upcoming Events — what's coming in the next two weeks.
  • Weather Alerts — color-coded banners when the National Weather Service issues a warning for Hawaii or a Pacific partner island.
  • Partner Island Times — small ticker showing the current time on Guam, Pohnpei, Pago Pago, etc.

Notification Bell

The bell in the top bar shows a red badge when something new arrives. Click it for a dropdown of recent notifications: new signups, new website submissions, new chat messages, event signup requests. Click any notification to jump to it.

Tip. The bell only counts items you haven't seen yet. Open them once and the badge clears.

Presence Strip

The row of avatars in the top bar shows who is logged in right now. A green dot means online, a yellow dot means idle (no activity for 10+ minutes), no dot means offline. Hover any avatar for the person's name. This is also how you start a chat with a coworker — see the next section.

Chat with Staff

The blue chat bubble in the bottom-left opens the staff chat panel. You can:

  • Send a direct message to any other logged-in staff member
  • Share a photo or screenshot
  • Start a screen-share if you need someone to see what you're looking at
  • Scroll back through earlier messages (use "Load older messages" to fetch more)

Chat works on a phone or iPad too — the panel becomes a full-screen overlay on smaller devices.

Daily Email Report

Every weekday morning at 6:00 AM Hawaii time, an automated email goes out to the staff on the Daily Report Recipient List. It summarizes:

  • Today's classes or events with confirmed and pending counts
  • Yesterday's attendance
  • New website submissions waiting for a reply
  • Anything else that needs eyes-on action

Open the email on your phone before your first cup of coffee and you already know the day. (To change who gets it, an admin uses the Recipient Lists section.)

Managing Interactions

An Interaction is one logged conversation with a contact: a phone call, an email, an in-person visit, anything. Logging interactions builds the family's history so when a colleague picks up the phone next month they can see what's already happened.

Create one

  1. Open the Interactions tab.
  2. Click New Interaction.
  3. Pick the contact (or create a new one).
  4. Choose the channel (Phone, Email, In Person, etc.) and the type (Information, Referral, Follow-up, etc.).
  5. Add notes about what happened. Set status: Open, Draft, Needs Review, or Closed.
  6. Click Save.

Add follow-up notes later

Click any interaction in the list to open it. Add a new note (up to 500 characters) — your name and the date stamp automatically. The conversation history stays threaded so anyone can read it later.

Filter and find

Use the filters above the list: by status, by date range, by contact, by channel, by type. The search box also works. Hit Export CSV to download whatever you're looking at as a spreadsheet.

Website Submissions

The Website Data section shows everything families and visitors submit through forms on the public website. Tabs across the top split it by submission type:

  • Provider Requests — service providers asking to be listed
  • Anti-Bullying Pledges — pledge form sign-ups
  • Event Requests — community groups asking LDAH to host an event
  • Contact Messages — the regular contact form

Each submission is a card. Click to expand it. Use the status buttons to advance it through its workflow. Add admin notes if you need to. Archive it when handled. Export to CSV anytime.

Viewing Event Signups

Open Events & Programs from the CMS sidebar, then click any event card. Click View Signups to open the signup list.

One-time events

You'll see a flat list of everyone who registered, grouped by status (New, Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled). Each row is a card you can click to expand and see all of their info.

Recurring programs (like Connect-Gen, Learning Lab)

You'll see the next 30 days of sessions, each with its own signup count. Each session can be cancelled individually with a checkbox (good for holidays or schedule changes). Cancelled sessions show a red strikethrough.

Each signup card has three info boxes

  • Personal Info — contact, address, demographics, accommodations
  • Student & Program — student info, sessions selected, care coordination
  • Audit Log — who changed what and when on this signup

Marking Attendance

For events that have already happened, the signups list switches into attendance mode. Each signup gets a checkbox — tick it if the family showed up. The system saves automatically.

For Connect-Gen and other programs that mix in-person and virtual on different dates, you can also use a per-date status override (Confirmed In-Person vs. Confirmed Virtual) right in the signup card's status dropdown.

Why this matters. Attendance triggers the post-event feedback email automatically (see Reminder Schedule below). It also feeds the daily report and the Event Summary panel.

Quick CMS Updates

To update text or a photo on the public website, open Page Editor from the CMS sidebar.

  1. Click the page tab at the top (Home, Contact, Volunteer, etc.).
  2. Click directly on any text or photo on the page.
  3. Make your change. Use the toolbar (B, I, U, list, link, color, size).
  4. Watch for the green "Saved" indicator — your change is auto-saved as a draft.
  5. When you're ready, click Publish to push the change to the live website.

If you need to undo a draft you haven't published yet, click Discard. See the full reference for more.

Tier 3 — Full Reference

Dashboard & KPIs

The Dashboard is the home screen. It is built around KPI tiles, panels, and live widgets.

KPI tiles

Each tile shows a count and is clickable — clicking jumps you to the matching list. Common tiles include:

  • New Pledges, Volunteer Apps, Provider Requests, Event Requests, Contact Messages
  • Active Events, Recurring Programs, Active FAQs, Community Resources
  • Team Members, Board Members

Today's Session Sheet

If anything is happening today, this panel shows it: program name, date, location, confirmed count, pending count. Click into any session for details.

Recent Interactions

The last ten interactions, with the contact name, type, and the staff member who logged it. Click any row to open it.

Weather Alerts

Color-coded banners appear when the National Weather Service issues an alert for Hawaii or a Pacific partner island. Severity colors: red (extreme/severe), amber (moderate), gray (minor/unknown). Click the X to dismiss; updated alerts will re-appear if the content changes. Configure which alert types you want to see in your Profile.

Partner Island Times

A small ticker at the top of the dashboard shows the current local time for American Samoa, Guam, CNMI, FSM, Marshall Islands, and Palau. Helpful for staff supporting partner sites.

Contacts

The Contacts section is the master list of every person LDAH has interacted with. Contacts are auto-created from event signups, registrations, and form submissions, and can also be added manually.

What's stored

  • Name, email, phone, organization, location
  • Grant or program affiliation (if any)
  • Demographics (when collected via registration form)
  • How they first found LDAH
  • A timeline of every interaction, signup, and email sent to them

Working with a contact

Click any name to open the contact panel. You'll see the full timeline — interactions, signups, emails sent. Click any item in the timeline for the full record. Use the Archive button to remove inactive contacts from the active list (they stay queryable; nothing is deleted).

SuperAdmin / Admin only. Adding and editing contacts directly is restricted to admins. Most staff create contacts implicitly by logging an interaction or registering a family.

Interactions

The full Interactions list with filters, sort, and CSV export. See the Daily Use section above for the basics.

What counts as an Interaction

Anything where you communicated with a contact: a phone call, an email reply, a walk-in visit, a follow-up text. The point is to build a history so the next staff member who picks up that contact knows what's happened.

Status flow

StatusMeaning
DraftYou started but haven't finished writing it up.
OpenActive — needs follow-up or further action.
Needs ReviewFlagged for someone else (often a supervisor) to read.
ClosedFinished — no further action expected.

Slide-out detail panel

Clicking a row opens a side panel with the full interaction, threaded notes, and a small audit trail. You can add new notes from here without leaving the main list.

CMS Dashboard

The CMS section starts with a CMS Dashboard — a bird's-eye view of all website-content KPIs (Team count, Resources count, Active Events, Active FAQs, etc.). Quick-action buttons let you add a Team Member, create an Event, add a Resource, or add a FAQ without navigating elsewhere first.

Team & Board

Two tabs:

  • Team Members — staff with name, title, phone, email, photo, bio
  • Board Members — board with name, title, photo, bio (no phone/email)

Add new entries with the + Add button. Edit any card by clicking it. Drag cards to reorder. Archive entries instead of deleting them.

Photo tips. Square photos work best. Maximum 5 MB. The photo appears on the public Who We Are page within seconds of upload.

Gallery Management

Two galleries on the public site:

  • Gallery 1 — Who We Are page
  • Gallery 2 — Volunteer page

Upload photos (max 5 MB each), drag to reorder, archive when done. The order in the CMS matches the order shown on the website.

Community Resources

The Community Resources directory is what families see when they search for support providers. Each resource has:

  • Name, type, description of services
  • City, island (or Statewide for resources available everywhere)
  • Website URL, logo

The Services description is searchable by families on the public site, so write it in plain language. Use the search box at the top of the CMS list to filter, and Export CSV to share with partners.

FAQ Management

FAQs are organized in collapsible categories. Each category can be reordered by dragging. Inside each category, FAQs can be reordered too.

Adding an FAQ

  1. Click + Add FAQ.
  2. Pick a category (or add a new one).
  3. Type the question.
  4. Type the answer. Use the formatting toolbar for bold, italic, links, lists.
  5. Optionally add Action Steps — these appear below the answer as a numbered list.
  6. Save.

Archive instead of delete. CSV export includes the action steps as separate rows, which makes them easier to review in a spreadsheet.

Events & Programs

Two kinds of things live here:

  • Events — one-time things on a specific date (workshops, fairs, special talks).
  • Programs (a.k.a. Recurring Events) — repeat on a weekly or monthly pattern (Connect-Gen, Learning Lab).

Creating an event

  1. Click + Create Event.
  2. Fill in title, description, image or PDF flyer (max 10 MB).
  3. Set the three dates: Start Showing (when families can see it), Move to Past (when signups close), Remove (when it disappears entirely from Past Events).
  4. Add Signup Date/Time options (the dates families can actually choose from).
  5. Add any Custom Questions you want to ask on the signup form.
  6. Toggle the Individual / Group field if you want to ask whether they're attending alone or with a group.
  7. Save. The event becomes visible on the public site at its Start Showing date.

Creating a program

Same form, plus a Schedules section. Add one schedule per cadence:

  • Weekly — pick a day of the week (e.g. every Monday), start time, end time, location, venue.
  • Monthly — pick the Nth occurrence (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Monday), start time, end time, location, venue.

You can add as many schedules as you need. Connect-Gen, for example, has four: Mondays virtual, Thursdays in-person on Oahu, 2nd Thursdays in Hilo, 3rd Thursdays in Kona. The public Events page renders all of these as separate lines on the program card.

Signups & Sessions

Each event or program has its own signup list. The list combines registrations from the public LDAH website, the LDAH mobile app, and (for some programs) the legacy Google Form imports.

Sorting and filtering

Signups sort by status by default (New → Pending → Confirmed → Cancelled). You can switch to chronological. Use the filter buttons across the top to narrow by status, by session date, or by displaced (rescheduled) status.

Each signup card shows

  • Header — name, status badge, registration type pill, attendance pill, source pill (e.g. "via announcement")
  • Personal Info box — contact, address, demographics, military status, accommodations
  • Student & Program box — student info, sessions selected, care coordination
  • Audit Log box — every change made to this signup, color-coded by who and when

Common actions

  • Status dropdown — change New / Pending / Confirmed / Cancelled
  • Change Date — move this signup to a different session (great for "can I switch from Monday to Thursday?")
  • Add Session — add another date to a multi-date signup
  • Archive — move this signup off the active list (it stays for reporting)
  • Resend confirmation email — from the Email Log row for this signup

Cancelled sessions

For programs, each upcoming date has a checkbox in the signups view. Tick it to cancel just that one date (e.g. holiday). The date shows red strikethrough. People who had signed up for that date appear in a "Displaced Signups" panel so you can reschedule them.

Attendance & Feedback

After a session date passes, the signups view automatically switches into attendance mode. Each signup gets a checkbox — tick it if the family attended.

Per-date overrides for mixed mode

Connect-Gen sometimes runs the same person as virtual on Mondays and in-person on Thursdays. Use the per-date status dropdown on each signup card to override their default mode for one date only. The card displays a small "mode override" pill so you know it's set.

Feedback emails

Marking attendance triggers the post-event feedback email automatically. If a participant doesn't fill it out within a few days, a follow-up email goes out (also automatically). All sent emails are logged in the Email Log.

Volunteer Management

Two tabs:

  • Opportunities — the volunteer roles you're recruiting for. Title, description, image, requirements, dates. Toggle "Show always" for ongoing needs.
  • Applications — people who applied. Each application has a status flow: New → Contacted → Interviewing → Accepted / Declined.

Add admin notes on any application. CSV export groups applications by opportunity.

Website Data

Already covered in Tier 2. Each tab has its own status flow:

Event RequestsPending → Reviewed → Approved → Scheduled → Completed/Declined

Email System

LDAH sends a lot of automated email through the dashboard. Most of it happens behind the scenes — you don't have to do anything for it to fire. Here's what gets sent and when:

Email typeTriggered by
Signup confirmationAnyone signing up for an event or program
5-day reminderSent 5 days before each session, daily 4 PM HST cron
1-day reminderSent 1 day before each session
Cancellation / reschedule noticeCancelling a signup or moving its date
Event-cancelled noticeCancelling an entire session date for everyone
Post-event feedbackMarking a signup as attended
Feedback follow-upIf feedback hasn't been submitted within ~3 days
Recording & slidesManually fired from the event card after a session
Announcement blastManually fired when an event is ready to promote
Resource Partner update reminderTwice yearly + auto-nudges 7 and 14 days later
Volunteer application acknowledgmentSubmitting a volunteer application (deferred)

Email Log

Open Logs → Email Log to see every email the system has sent. Each row shows recipient, subject, type, send date, and status. Click any row for the full message preview — you can see exactly what the family received. Use the Resend button on any row to re-deliver the same email.

Senders

Most emails sign as Leilani Kailiawa from registration@ldahawaii.org. Resource Partner Update emails sign as La'a Salvani from LSalvani@. Different workflows have different sender personas — that's intentional.

Recipient Lists

Some emails go to staff, not families. The Recipient Lists CMS card lets superAdmins manage who's on each list:

  • Daily Report Recipients — who gets the 6 AM HST daily summary
  • Resource Partner Recipients — staff CC'd on partner update workflow emails

Reminder Schedule

The Logs → Reminder Schedule page shows every upcoming reminder email the system plans to send, organized by event session, with separate sections for 5-day reminders, 1-day reminders, and feedback follow-ups. The cron runs daily at 4 PM Hawaii time and picks up everything due in the next 24 hours.

This page is your safety net — scan it once a week to confirm reminders are scheduled for the sessions you expect.

Zoom Defaults card

Open Settings → Zoom Defaults to update the Zoom meeting link, ID, and password used in all virtual reminder emails. Update this card when the Zoom meeting credentials rotate.

Recording & Slides

After a session is over and you have the Zoom recording link and slide deck PDF:

  1. Open the event card in CMS.
  2. Click Send Recording & Slides.
  3. Paste the Zoom recording URL.
  4. Upload the slides PDF (the file uploads to Storage and auto-prunes after 15 days).
  5. Pick which signups should receive it (defaults to everyone marked attended).
  6. Click Send.

The email goes out and is logged. If a family asks to be re-sent later, find the row in Email Log and click Resend — the attachments are remembered.

Announcement Blasts

When you want to promote an event to the broader contact list:

  1. Open the event card.
  2. Click Send Announcement.
  3. Pick recipients (defaults to all subscribed contacts; the system excludes anyone already signed up for this event so they don't get a "sign up" pitch for something they're already on).
  4. Preview the email.
  5. Send.

The "Sign Up" button in the announcement deep-links to the public signup form with the family's contact info pre-filled. Easier on families = more signups.

Resource Partner Updates

Twice a year LDAH asks every Community Resource partner to confirm their information is still accurate. The dashboard runs that workflow end-to-end:

  1. Admin starts an update cycle.
  2. Each partner gets an email (signed by La'a Salvani) with a public link to confirm or update their listing.
  3. If a partner hasn't responded after 7 days, a nudge email goes out automatically. Another at 14 days.
  4. Each partner's response (or non-response) shows on the Resource Update dashboard so you can see the cycle's progress.

Signup Source Tracking

Every signup records where it came from:

  • announcement-email — the family clicked Sign Up in an announcement blast
  • registration-email — they came in through a registration link
  • (no pill) — direct signup from the website or app

The signup card shows a small colored pill so you can see at a glance how someone arrived. The Event Summary panel rolls these up into conversion KPIs (e.g. "27% of announcement recipients signed up").

Page Editor (WYSIWYG)

WYSIWYG = "What You See Is What You Get". The Page Editor lets you change text and photos on the public website without writing any code. You see the page roughly the way it appears live, click directly on what you want to change, edit it, and publish.

How it works

  1. Pick a page tab at the top.
  2. Hover any element. A teal border appears around editable items.
  3. Click the element. A small editor opens.
  4. Make your edit. The change is auto-saved as a draft — you'll see a green "Saved" indicator.
  5. To go live, click Publish at the top. To throw away a draft, click Discard.

Drafts vs. live

Saved in the editor is not the same as live on the website. Always click Publish when you're ready for visitors to see the change. The "Unpublished Changes" indicator at the top tells you when there are drafts waiting.

Photos

Click any photo, then Change Photo. Pick a new image (max 5 MB). The photo uploads and saves automatically.

Pacific sub-pages

The Pacific section in the page list expands to show six island sub-pages (American Samoa, Guam, CNMI, Micronesia, Marshall Islands, Palau). Click Pacific first to expand the list, then pick the island you want.

Partner role limits

Staff with the Partner role can only edit their assigned island's page — they can't touch the rest of the site.

Training Videos

The Training Videos CMS lets admins post short walkthrough videos that staff can watch from the dashboard.

To add a video

  1. Upload the video to Google Drive (any LDAH Google Drive will work).
  2. In Google Drive, right-click the file → Share → set to "Anyone with the link". Copy the link.
  3. In the dashboard's Training Videos section, click + Add Video.
  4. Paste the link, give it a title and a one-sentence description, and pick a category.
  5. Save.

Change Requests

Some fields are protected — staff with editor permissions can request a change but not make it directly. The request goes into a queue. An Admin or SuperAdmin reviews it, approves or rejects, and the change (if approved) gets recorded in the Audit Log.

User Roles SuperAdmin

Seven roles control what each staff member can see and do:

RoleWhat they can do
SuperAdminEverything. Manage users, change roles, run audits, manage system lists.
AdminMost things. No user-role management.
WebAdminCMS for the public website. No internal admin tools.
AppAdminSame as WebAdmin but scoped to mobile-app content.
DailyUserDay-to-day staff: interactions, signups, website data.
DataUserRead-only views of reports, contacts, signups. Can't modify.
PartnerPacific island partners — limited Page Editor for their own island only.

Adding a user

Click + Add User. Enter their LDAH email, display name, role, and assigned location. The system sends them an invite. Archive instead of deleting when someone leaves — their interaction history stays attached.

Live status indicators

Each user row shows a status dot: green (online now), yellow (idle 10+ minutes), gray (offline), red (archived).

List Management SuperAdmin

Manage the dropdown options used throughout the dashboard:

  • Channels — the list of "How did you contact them?" options on Interactions (Phone, Email, In Person, etc.)
  • Interaction Types — Information, Referral, Follow-up, etc.
  • Grants & Programs — affiliations on Contacts
  • Titles / Positions and Departments — for User Profiles
  • Sidebar Tips — the rotating tips shown at the bottom of the sidebar

Add, rename, or archive any item. Changes are immediate.

Audit Log

Every change made through the dashboard is recorded with: who did it, when, what changed (old value → new value). The Audit Log is read-only — you can't edit or delete entries.

Color coding

Entries are color-coded by category so you can scan quickly: signups (teal), CMS edits (purple), user role changes (red), email sends (blue), etc.

Filtering

Filter by user, by date range, by category, or by signup ID. Useful when something looks wrong and you want to trace what happened.

Web/App Analytics Dashboard

The Analytics Dashboard shows what people do on the public LDAH website and mobile app. Metrics tracked:

  • Unique Visitors, Page Views
  • Phone-call clicks, Email clicks, Outbound link clicks
  • Modal Opens, Document/PDF Views
  • Form Submissions

Filter by time range. Drill into per-page breakdowns. Export to CSV or PDF.

Reports

Two report builders:

  • Interaction Reports — by date, by contact, by status, by type. CSV.
  • Custom Exports — pick any combination of fields and filters. CSV.

For session-level reports, look in the View Signups view of any program — there's a per-session report button there too.

Profile & Settings

Click your avatar in the top-right to open Profile. Editable fields:

  • Display Name, Email
  • Title, Department, Year Started
  • Phone, Bio
  • Skills
  • Emergency Contact (Name + Phone)
  • Profile Photo (max 2 MB, square works best)
  • Location / Island (controls which weather you see and which alerts apply to you)
  • Weather Alert Preferences (which alert types you want to see on the dashboard)

Resource Activity

Resource Activity is the file library — PDFs, Word docs, Excel sheets, PowerPoints, CSVs, etc. Max 25 MB per file. Upload, view, download, filter, export a summary list.
