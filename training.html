One job, start to finish: create the event, print the sign, let people scan, watch the numbers arrive.

Two buttons

Pink is backwards. Green is forwards.

Something still to come
The date is in the future. You get a QR flyer so people can sign up before and during.
The form · top

Name, date, presenter

The date defaults to today. Change it to the future date, or your event is not upcoming and you will not get a usable sign.

The form · where and what

Location prints on the sign. Fill it in.

The description also lands on the printed sign, word for word. Write it for a stranger walking past, not for the file.

The form · leave these empty

Everything below is for afterwards

Attendees and headcount stay empty. The QR signups fill them in for you. Typing a guess here now means counting people twice later.
Saved

A green UPCOMING card appears

Green tag, and a QR flyer button underneath. The pink one-off cards next to it have no such button — that is the difference you can see at a glance.

The sign

The flyer builds itself

Every word on it came out of the form you just filled in. Wrong on the form, wrong on the sign — so check it before you print.

The sign

The code, and a backup for phones that will not scan

Older phones sometimes cannot read a code. Read them the grey line instead — it goes to the same page.

The sign

Print it — the day before

Print it big

A full sheet stood up in a photo frame or a table tent beats a small one taped flat to the cloth.

Print two

One for each end of the table, so a queue does not form at one corner.

Or save the PDF

Print to PDF and send it ahead to whoever is setting up, if you are not the one travelling.

On the day

They sign themselves up in about a minute

Signed up

Just the camera app

No app to download. Point the normal camera at the code and tap the link that pops up.

They type, not you

Their name and details go in on their own phone. Nothing to transcribe later, nothing misspelled.

Works while you talk

One person scans while you are still talking to the next. The table does not back up.

Afterwards

The summary counted itself

Print / Save as PDF gives you the funder-ready copy. Edit Summary is where you add the materials you handed out and anything the numbers missed.

Passing it on

Three things to say slowly

Green before, pink after

The two buttons sit next to each other and do opposite jobs. This is the one people get wrong.

The sign mirrors the form

Name, date, location and description print exactly as typed. Check before printing, not at the booth.

Leave attendees empty

The scans do the counting. A number typed in advance turns into people counted twice.

Let them practise on a throwaway event first. Have each person create one called “Test — [their name]”, dated a few weeks out, and print the sign. Once the shape of it makes sense, they can do the real one. A practice event with nobody signed up costs nothing.
Recap

Five things to remember

Green = has not happened. Pink = already did.
Set the future date — it defaults to today
Location and description print on the sign
Leave attendees and headcount empty
Print the flyer the day before, not the morning of
