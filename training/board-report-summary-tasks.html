Your job: find the dashes and send them back to the presenter.

Step 1 · Find the report

Reports → Hawaii PTI Board Report

Super Admin only. If the card is missing, ask Daniel.

Step 2 · Pick the scope

It always opens on LDAH — switch to PIP

A board report never opens on partner data by accident. You choose PIP each time.

Pacific presenters: click PIP in the Showing switch.

Step 3 · Set the dates

Choose the reporting period

Showing: PIPThe switch turns purple.
From and ToCalendar icon, arrows move a month at a time.
Quick RangeCommon periods without the clicking.
To = todayCatch up from the start of the period.
Step 4 · Let it load

Wait for the Generated time stamp

PIP scope: partner work only, not a Hawaii PTI board figure.

Step 5 · Read the report

An orange dash means the box is empty

Number = filled in. Orange dash = nothing in the box.

Step 6 · Create summary tasks

Hover first — it tells you what it does

Shows you the list first. Nothing is written until you confirm.
No signups = a real zero, left alone. Already tasked = skipped.
Step 7 · Check the list

Read the number and where it came from

Check each number against its quote. Green tick = already sent.

Step 8 · Assign To

Choose who gets the task

Why you pick a nameThese sessions have a presenter name typed in but no account linked, so there is nobody to route to automatically.
Pick the presenterIn this example: Sandy Scanlan (partner).
Role in bracketsStaff and partners are both in the list.

Tick their sessions, then Create
