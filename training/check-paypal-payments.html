Checking PayPal payments against pending memberships

A two-minute walkthrough of the Membership Report and the Check PayPal tool — what it does, when to use it, and the one thing to remember about timing.

Weekly meeting · August 12, 2026 · Prepared by Oahu App Design

The big picture

How a membership becomes paid

1
Member joins online

They pick a level on the How to Help page. A membership record is created as Pending.

→
2
They pay with PayPal

The moment PayPal confirms the payment, our system marks the record Paid automatically.

→
3
Thank-you email goes out

The member automatically receives the LDAH thank-you with their member portal login.

Most of the time this happens on its own. The Membership Report is where we verify the ones that stay Pending — usually someone who started signing up but never finished paying.

Step 1 · Getting there

Main → Reports → Membership Reports

From the Main tab: click Reports in the sidebar, then the Membership Reports card.

Step 2 · Reading the report

Pending memberships are the ones to check

The counters up top show Paid vs Pending at a glance. Below, Pending Memberships lists each one — here, a $500 Sponsor from Aug 10 with no payment on record. Pending does not automatically mean they never paid. That is what we check next.

Step 3 · The Check PayPal button

One click asks PayPal directly

Check PayPal searches PayPal for payments matching the pending memberships. It matches on an ID PayPal keeps on every order — it never guesses by name or amount — and it changes nothing until you confirm.

Step 4 · Reading the result

Here is what it found — nothing

"No payment found" means PayPal has no record of this membership being paid — so the Pending status is correct, and our automatic reminder emails to that member simply continue. Nothing to do.

If a payment had been found, the report would show the match and offer to mark the membership Paid.

Step 5 · Marking a membership paid

Marking Paid emails the member — so it asks first

Whether from a PayPal match or the Mark Paid button, confirming shows:

Are you sure they paid?

Marking this membership Paid immediately sends the member the thank-you email with their member portal login.

Only super-admin accounts can mark a membership as paid.

Confirm only when the payment is verified — the email is real.

The one thing to remember

PayPal’s records can run up to 3 hours behind

A member can pay and still show as Pending for a little while — PayPal’s reporting simply lags.

If someone says “I just paid” and Check PayPal disagrees: wait, then check again later. Don’t ask them to pay twice, and don’t mark them Paid on their word alone.

Our reminder emails already tell members payments “can take up to three hours to show in our records,” so nobody who paid will be alarmed.

Recap

Four things to keep

1
Paid marking is automatic

When PayPal confirms a payment, the membership flips to Paid on its own — the report is for verifying, not day-to-day bookkeeping.

2
Check PayPal looks before it touches

It reports what it found — often “no payment found,” which is an answer, not a problem. Marking Paid always asks first, and sends the thank-you email.

3h
Respect the three-hour lag

A very recent payment may not show yet. Wait and re-check before treating a membership as unpaid.

4
Refunds get flagged, not reversed

A refund never cancels a membership automatically — it raises a note for staff, because a refund usually means a conversation is already happening.

Questions or odd cases: bring them to Daniel. Mahalo!
