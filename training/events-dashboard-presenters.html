Step 1 · The calendar

Admin → Events Dashboard

The legend counts it for you: assigned vs needs a presenter. Orange dropdowns are the to-do list.

Step 2 · Assigning

Pick a name right on the session

Saves instantly — no Save button, no page reload. Changing a presenter later is the same one click.

Step 3 · The presenter list

Names come from List Management → Presenters

Add Presenter puts a name in every dropdown. Sort sets the order. Archive retires a name without touching past assignments.

Step 4 · Adding an event

Click any open day — the plus is the door

Works on any day, busy or blank — hover the empty space and the + appears. Clicking it opens Add Event with the date pre-filled.

Step 5 · Pick a type

Three event types — pick one, the right editor opens

Screening = full screening-consent QR form · Remote Signup = RSVP event with seat counts (the movie nights) · Outreach Booth = simple QR signup at a table.

Step 6 · Event details

Basics, flyer, and the showing window

Event Date is when it happens; Start Showing / Move to Past / Remove Date control how long it stays visible.

Step 7 · The signup form builder

You choose the questions — Save generates the QR

The Screening checkbox adds the full consent section and feeds the Screenings Report. The kiosk QR is generated when you Save.
