Step 1 · Getting there

CMS → Home Rotation

The green counter — 5 in the rotation — is your at-a-glance answer to “what’s live right now?”

Step 2 · Ticking

Ticked = in the rotation. One click either way.

Green outline is in; orange outline is sitting out. One click on the checkbox moves it either way.

Step 3 · How it rotates

Two at a time — and everything gets its turn

Item A
Item B

Visitors see two at a time — on the website home page and in the app’s splash.

They’re not shown the same ones again until they’ve seen the rest — so every ticked item gets in front of people, no matter how many are in the rotation.

Step 4 · Reading the list

Types on the right, dates or “ongoing” on the left

Dated events show their date; standing items like Membership say ongoing and stay in until you untick them.
