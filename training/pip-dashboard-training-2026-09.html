Event Attendance, one-off event

Adding a new attendee to a one-off event

  • A new person needs a phone or an email. Without one, Create & add refuses: “Add a phone or an email — we can’t follow up with someone we can’t reach.”
  • Typos are caught: “n/a” is not an email, and a phone number needs at least 7 digits.
  • Details for reports (optional): ethnicity, child’s age range and gender, disability categories, military status, how they heard about LDAH, zip and island.
  • Please fill in what you know — these are exactly the numbers the PTI and demographics reports count.
No phone or email? Don’t add them. Put them in the bulk count at the very bottom of the event summary instead. No sense adding someone we can’t reach until we have more info.

Event Attendance, one-off event

Picking someone who has no phone or email

  • If the person you pick from the search has no phone and no email, they are not added yet.
  • Type a phone or an email and press Save & add — it is saved onto their contact and they join the list.
  • No phone or email for them? Press Cancel — it adds no one — and count them in the bulk count at the bottom of the summary instead.
  • People already on past events are left as they are; the rule applies when someone is added.

Event Attendance, one-off event

Updating attendees already on an event

  • Open any one-off event and use Update next to a name.
  • The panel shows what is on file: phone, email and all the report details. Change what you know and press Save to contact.
  • For these past entries, phone and email are optional — you can save demographics on their own.
  • Saving updates the contact and this event’s record, so the attendance reports agree.
This works for anyone who enters one-off events, including partners for their own events.

Reports

Missing Phone & Email report

  • Lists every active contact on your island with no phone and no email.
  • Click a name to open the contact card and add a phone or email. Press Refresh and they drop off the list.
  • Columns: type, island, date added, added by, and source.
Started at about 36–39 contacts when it launched on Sept 19 — mostly one-off quick-adds. Please fix the ones you added. From now on, people with no phone or email go in the bulk count, not the database.

Home, My Day

URGENT tasks

  • A task marked URGENT is pinned to the top of your list with a red border and an URGENT label, whatever its date.
  • It also counts toward the Overdue number at the top, which pulses until it is dealt with.
  • Used when something must happen today — for example, calling a family before a Connect-Gen deadline.
  • Close it the normal way once it is done.
Note: the bell does not ring for new assignments. Check My Day — that is where your tasks live.

Reports, Sandy only

For Sandy: the LDAH / PIP / Both switch

  • Six reports have a SHOWING switch: Interactions Summary, Contact Demographics, Event Attendance, Feedback, Membership and Certification.
  • PIP = all six Pacific islands. LDAH = the Hawaiʻi work. Both = everything together. Reports open on PIP.
  • The PTI Report (formerly the Hawaii PTI Board Report) is under Reports and you can open it.
Everyone else: your reports show only your own island, so you will not see this switch.

Behind the scenes

Fixed quietly — nothing to do, but good to know

Attendees keep the contact you pick

When you picked someone on a one-off event, the system used to re-match them by email and phone afterwards. People with no email lost their link, and people sharing an email were filed under the wrong person. That is fixed, and 56 attendees were re-linked — including Sandy’s own attendance, which had been counted under another person’s record.

Emails in the notes belong on the contact

If a family’s email or phone only appears in a note, copy it into the contact’s Email or Phone field. System emails only reach the address on the contact.
