This is separate from emailing the recording — here it becomes a permanent library video members can rewatch.

Step 1 · Publish

Click “Publish to member archive”

Admin → Zoom Platform Recordings. Find the session, then click Publish to member archive.

Step 2 · It’s published

Confirmed — and the upload begins

The button becomes a green In member archive tag, and a “Published” message confirms Google Drive is now processing it.

Step 3 · Recordings Archive

It lands in the Recordings Archive

Admin → Recordings Archive. Your session shows On the portal. Click Edit to refine it.

Step 4 · Edit the details

Title, description, and any slides

Title and Description are what members see. The cover image is optional — the event flyer carries over on its own, so only add one if you want something different. Under Files, Choose Files attaches slides or handouts (PDF, Word, PowerPoint). Then Save.

The one thing to expect

Give it 10–15 minutes to process

10–15
min

After you publish, the video is compressed and uploaded to Google Drive — that runs about 10 to 15 minutes.

During that time it may not play yet. That’s expected, not an error. Wait it out, then test.

Step 5 · Test on the portal

Confirm it plays in the Video Library

Open the Member Portal → Video Library and press Watch recording to confirm it plays. Members can now watch it any time.

Recap

The whole routine

1
Publish to member archive

Admin → Zoom Platform Recordings. Find the session and click Publish to member archive.

2
Edit the details

Admin → Recordings Archive → Edit. Set title & description; the flyer carries over (cover image optional); attach slides if you have them; Save.

⌛
Wait 10–15 minutes

The video compresses and uploads to Google Drive. It may not play right away — that’s expected.

3
Test on the portal

Open Member Portal → Video Library and press Watch recording to confirm it plays.
