LDAH Int — Admin training

Duplicate contacts: the 5 AM sweep and the Admin call

The system finds them. Admin decides.

The nightly sweep

5:00 AM, every morning

When

Before anyone signs in

A CRON job runs at 5:00 AM daily. New duplicates are waiting with the morning coffee.

What it checks

Shared phone numbers

Two cards on one number is the strongest everyday sign of a duplicate. A shared email makes it stronger.

What it never does

Merge on its own

It only raises the question. A person answers it, every time.

Step one

The morning task

Compare the two records opens the panel
Step two

Is this one person, or two?

Merging cannot be undone from here. Keeping them separate is remembered, so the pair is never raised again.
Step three

Read the two records

Record A survives. Record B is folded in and removed. The task even describes what this specific pair looks like.
The judgment call

One person, or a shared phone?

Merge if

It reads like one person

Same name and phone. A work email and a personal one. A surname that changed.

Keep both if

It reads like a household

Two parents, a couple, or staff on one office line — different first names on the same number.

If unsure

Keep both

A wrong keep-both just leaves two cards. A wrong merge can't be undone from the panel.

Outcome one

Green: same person — merge them

One card, whole history. The receipt counts what moved. This is the step that cannot be undone from the panel.
Outcome two

Dark: two different people — keep both

The cards

Nothing changes

Both records stay exactly as they are.

The memory

Never asked again

The pair is remembered, and the 5 AM job skips it from now on.

The task

Clears the same way

Keep-both is a completed review, not a skipped one.

Verify

One card, whole story

One record left. The closed Data review entry stays on the Activity Timeline — the decision is on the record.
Recap

Four things to remember

One

5 AM sweep

Shared phone numbers become Data review tasks. Nothing merges itself.

Two

Read both records

Record A survives; Record B folds in and is removed.

Three

Answer the question

Green merges. Dark keeps both — and is remembered.

Four

When in doubt

Keep both. You can merge later; you can't unmerge here.
