Step 1 · Find the recording

Admin → Zoom Platform Recordings

The recording appears here once Zoom finishes processing it. Don’t see it yet? Wait a bit and click Refresh — it’s still uploading, not lost.

Step 2 · Copy the link

Click “Copy link to send”

✓ Link copied

It opens the recording directly — no passcode needed — so it’s ready to paste straight into your email.

Step 3 · Open the event

Events & Programs → the session’s task

View Signups (blue) is the list of everyone who registered. The Send Recording & Slides task button (gold, at the bottom) is your reminder — click it to open the send window.

Step 4 · The send window

Two lists, already sorted for you

Attendees came · No-Shows registered but missed it, and get the “Sorry we missed you” version. Both are ticked by default — everyone who signed up is in.

Step 5 · Paste the link & add slides

Paste the link, then attach your slides

Paste the link into Zoom Recording URL (leave the passcode blank). Choose Files to attach your PowerPoint or handouts (PDF/JPG). The session auto-saves to the member archive after you send. Then hit Send.

2

Events & Programs → the session’s Send Recording task / View Signups — the list of everyone who registered.

3
Paste & send to both groups

Follow the signup-sheet routine, paste the link, and send to attendees and no-shows alike.

i
