Sharing a screen shot in Team Messages
LDAH-Int · September 2026
New in Team Messages

Send a screen shot, not a paragraph

Capture what is on your screen, paste it into the message box, press Send. Your teammate sees the picture in the thread.

Why it helps

Showing beats describing

Reporting a problem

Send IT_Help the actual error instead of typing out what it said. Half the back and forth disappears.

Pointing at a button

"The orange one on the right" becomes a picture with the button plainly in it.

Checking a record

Show a teammate the contact card or the report you are looking at before anyone edits anything.

Step 1

Open the conversation first

Click the teammate's name in the list. Everything after this happens in that thread.

Step 2 · Windows

Clip part of the screen

Windows+ Shift+ S

What happens

The screen dims and the pointer becomes a crosshair. Drag a box around the part you want and let go.

Where it goes

Straight onto your clipboard, ready to paste. A small notification appears if you also want to save or mark it up.

If nothing happens

Open the Snipping Tool from the Start menu and click New. Same result, one more click.

Windows · the rest of the set

Three more you may want

Press thisYou get
Print ScreenThe whole screen, copied to the clipboard.
Alt + Print ScreenOnly the window you are working in, copied to the clipboard.
Windows + Print ScreenThe whole screen saved as a file in Pictures → Screenshots.

The key is often labelled PrtScn or Prt Sc, usually at the top right of the keyboard.

Step 2 · Mac

Clip part of the screen

Command+ Shift+ 4

What happens

The pointer becomes a crosshair. Drag a box around the part you want and let go. Press Esc to back out.

Where it goes

Saved as a file on your Desktop, and a thumbnail floats in the corner for a few seconds. Click it to mark it up.

Straight to the clipboard

Hold Control as well — Control, Command, Shift, 4 — and it copies instead of saving a file.

Mac · the rest of the set

Three more you may want

Press thisYou get
Command + Shift + 3The whole screen, saved to the Desktop.
Command + Shift + 4, then SpaceA camera pointer — click any window to capture just that window.
Command + Shift + 5A toolbar with all the options, a timer, and a setting for where files are saved.

Adding Control to any of these copies to the clipboard instead of saving a file.

Worth knowing

Clipboard or file — both work

On the clipboard

Nothing appears anywhere. It is being held, invisibly, until you paste it. Fastest route: capture, click the message box, paste, send.

Saved as a file

A picture lands on your Desktop or in your Pictures folder. Useful when you want to send it later, or to more than one person.

Rule of thumb

Sending it right now? Use the clipboard. Keeping it for a while? Save the file.

Step 3

Paste it into the message box

Windows: Ctrl + V Mac: Command + V Add a line of text, then Send
Step 4

It lands in the thread

Your name, the time, the picture, and whatever you wrote with it. Click the picture to open it full size.

Two habits

File it right, capture only what you need

Link to client

About a specific family or case? Choose them here before sending, and the exchange files with their record instead of sitting in general chat.

Crop tight

These messages are logged for audit. Capture the one panel you are asking about — not the whole screen with another family's name on it.
