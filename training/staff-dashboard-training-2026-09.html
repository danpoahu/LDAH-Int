Home, My Day

One case, one card

  • A family’s case file now shows only under My open cases — never a second time in Things to do today.
  • When a case follow-up is due, its card gets an orange FOLLOW-UP TODAY or red FOLLOW-UP OVERDUE badge.
  • Do not close a case card to “tick it off” — closing it ends the advocacy and starts the document countdown.
  • Emails and calls you log about the family are separate to-dos. Closing those is safe and does not end the case.
  • When Noelani assigns an advocate by changing the task’s owner, the card is renamed “Case advocacy — advocating for …” and its follow-up moves out 3 months automatically.
Who: Every advocate, and Noelani for the families still waiting to be assigned.

Home, My Day

URGENT tasks

  • A task marked URGENT is pinned to the top of your list with a red border and an URGENT label, whatever its date.
  • It also counts toward the Overdue number at the top, which pulses until it is dealt with.
  • Used when something must happen today — for example, calling a family before a Connect-Gen deadline.
  • Close it the normal way once it is done.
Note: the bell does not ring for new assignments. Check My Day — that is where your tasks live.

Missing Phone & Email report

  • Lists every active contact with no phone and no email.
  • Click a name to open the contact card and add a phone or email. Press Refresh and they drop off the list.
  • Columns: type, island, date added, added by, and source.
  • Partners see their own island’s contacts; admins see LDAH; Super Admins and Sandy can switch.
Started at about 36–39 contacts when it launched on Sept 19 — mostly one-off quick-adds. Fix your own. From now on, people with no phone or email go in the bulk count at the bottom of the summary, not the database.

Event Attendance, one-off event

Adding a new attendee to a one-off event

  • A new person needs a phone or an email. Without one, Create & add refuses: “Add a phone or an email — we can’t follow up with someone we can’t reach.”
  • Typos are caught: “n/a” is not an email, and a phone number needs at least 7 digits.
  • Details for reports (optional): ethnicity, child’s age range and gender, disability categories, military status, how they heard about LDAH, zip and island.
  • Please fill in what you know — these are exactly the numbers the PTI and demographics reports count.
No phone or email? Don’t add them. Put them in the bulk count at the very bottom of the event summary instead. No sense adding someone we can’t reach until we have more info.

Event Attendance, one-off event

Picking someone who has no phone or email

  • If the person you pick from the search has no phone and no email, they are not added yet.
  • Type a phone or an email and press Save & add — it is saved onto their contact and they join the list.
  • No phone or email for them? Press Cancel — it adds no one — and count them in the bulk count at the bottom of the summary instead.
  • People already on past events are left as they are; the rule applies when someone is added.

Event Attendance, one-off event

Updating attendees already on an event

  • Open any one-off event and use Update next to a name.
  • The panel shows what is on file: phone, email and all the report details. Change what you know and press Save to contact.
  • For these past entries, phone and email are optional — you can save demographics on their own.
  • Saving updates the contact and this event’s record, so the attendance reports agree.
This is for anyone who enters one-off events, including partners for their own events.

Contacts, Edit contact

Archiving a contact

  • Open a contact, choose Edit, then Archive contact.
  • You are asked first: the person will disappear from Contacts, the attendee search and reports. Nothing is deleted.
  • Keep contact backs out. Yes, archive archives.
  • Who did it and when is recorded, and it appears in the audit log.
Who: Super Admins and admins.

Contacts

The Archived view & Restore

  • Tick Archived on the Contacts toolbar to see archived contacts. Untick to go back.
  • Archived by and Archived on show who and when (“before tracking” = archived before this existed).
  • A green ✓ Active record on file means this was a duplicate and the person is still with us — click it to open the active contact.
  • Restore brings a contact back in one click.
Who: Super Admins and admins.

Behind the scenes

Fixed quietly — nothing to do, but good to know

Attendees keep the contact you pick

When you picked someone on a one-off event, the system used to re-match them by email and phone afterwards. People with no email lost their link, and people sharing an email were filed under the wrong person. Fixed, and 56 attendees were re-linked — including Sandy’s own attendance, which had been counted under another person’s record.

Duplicate records

When two records may be the same person, a “Possible duplicate” task shows both side by side with Same person — merge them or Two different people — keep both. Read both records first; merging cannot be undone from the dashboard.

Emails in the notes belong on the contact

If a family’s email or phone only appears in a note, copy it into the contact’s Email / Phone fields. System emails only reach the address on the contact.

Supporting the Pacific partners

They got their own training

Pacific partners have a shorter version covering only what they can do: adding and updating one-off attendees, the Missing Phone & Email report, URGENT tasks, and a Sandy-only slide on the switch and PTI Report. They were told: no phone or email means the bulk count, not a new contact.

What they can’t do

Partners cannot archive or restore contacts, and the Case Advocacy report is admin only. Their reports show only their own island — except Sandy, who can switch to LDAH or Both.

Questions come to Noelani

Their training names Noelani as the contact. Most of their Missing Phone & Email list is American Samoa quick-adds from one-off gatherings — a good first thing to follow up on.
